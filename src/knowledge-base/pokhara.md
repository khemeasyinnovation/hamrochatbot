# Pokhara Overview

Pokhara is one of Nepal's fastest-growing cities, known for tourism, lakeside views, and rising land demand. It sits in Kaski district and serves as a gateway to the Annapurna region, drawing both domestic and international visitors year-round.

# Lakeside Area

Lakeside (Phewa Tal area) is Pokhara's primary tourist hub. Land prices here are among the highest in the city due to hotel, guesthouse, and restaurant demand. Investment in Lakeside land often targets hospitality use rather than residential.

# Mahendrapool Area

Mahendrapool is closer to Pokhara's city center, offering a mix of residential and commercial property. It's popular for family homes and small businesses due to proximity to markets and schools.

# Buying Land in Nepal - FAQ

Foreign nationals generally cannot directly own land in Nepal; land ownership is restricted to Nepali citizens except in specific investment cases. Buyers should verify land ownership documents (lalpurja) and confirm the property is free of disputes before purchase. A registered lawyer or land broker is typically involved in the transaction and registration process.
